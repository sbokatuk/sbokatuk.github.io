SIM Bridge

What it does

Everything the adapter's SIM cards can do, on the phone in your pocket.

  • Calls

    • Make and answer calls through any SIM in any adapter, with voice over Bluetooth
    • Calls go through Android's own call system: answer from the lock screen, a Bluetooth headset, your car or a smartwatch
    • Recent calls, missed-call notifications, a dialer with SIM choice and tone dialing

  • SMS

    • Send and receive SMS, long messages included, in conversations with search
    • One-tap “Copy code” on verification SMS, “Mark as read” from the notification
    • A message that failed can be sent again without being sent twice

  • Adapters and SIM cards

    • Several adapters connected at the same time, each with its own SIM cards
    • Signal, network and battery of every adapter in the top bar, like the phone's status bar
    • Every network named by brand and country, with its logo
    • A card for every SIM: its name, number and operator follow the SIM to whichever adapter or phone slot it moves to

  • Made to stay connected

    • Pairs through Android's device picker and keeps the link up with the screen off
    • Reconnects by itself when an adapter comes back in range
    • Follows the phone's flight mode

  • Xiaomi watch

    • On Xiaomi Vela watches (Watch S5), a companion watch app shows the adapters' signal and battery
    • Answer or decline a call on the watch
    • Any watch, headset or car connected to the phone sees the calls like any other call

  • Private by design

    • No account, no ads, no analytics
    • The app has no internet permission: your messages, calls and contacts stay on your phone
    • Contacts are read in place to show names, never copied. Privacy policy

Supported adapters

You need a compatible Bluetooth SIM adapter with a SIM card, and an Android phone (Android 8.0 or newer).

  • IKOS K7

    • Calls and SMS
    • Wi-Fi hotspot, mobile data and APN settings

  • IKOS K7S

    • Calls and SMS
    • Wi-Fi hotspot, mobile data and APN settings

  • IKOS K1S

    • Calls and SMS
    • The adapter's button works as a remote camera shutter

SIM Bridge works with the same adapters as the SIMPlus and SIMPlus+ apps. It is an independent app and is not affiliated with IKOS or the developers of SIMPlus.

Questions

Do I need another app as well?

No. SIM Bridge connects to the adapter by itself. An adapter keeps one connection at a time, so if another app is connected to it, disconnect or forget the adapter there first.

Does SIM Bridge use the internet?

No. The app has no internet permission. SMS and calls go through the adapter's SIM card and your mobile operator; everything the app stores stays on your phone.

Can I use several adapters?

Yes. Connect several adapters at the same time; each shows its own SIM cards, signal and battery, and you choose which SIM to use.

Will my watch or car answer the calls?

Yes. Calls go through Android's call system, so a Bluetooth headset, car or smartwatch connected to the phone rings and can answer, as with any other call. On Xiaomi Vela watches the SIM Bridge watch app also shows the adapters' signal and battery.

Which languages and phones?

English and Russian, light and dark theme. It works on foldables, including the Xiaomi MIX Flip cover screen.

How much does it cost?

SIM Bridge is free, with no ads.
